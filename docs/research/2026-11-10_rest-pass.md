## Process

### Step 1: the record

- `luthi/v2/day_record.py`. Each living layer can keep an append-only list of experiences: the moment, the salience, the context vector and the mean input pattern. No weight snapshot, no capacity, no eviction.
- **Switched on at run time** by `enable_day_record(model)`, the way `set_wake_attenuation` is, and not by a constructor argument. No run's recorded configuration changes meaning, and `rebuild_run` needs nothing new.
- **What is recorded.** A moment is recorded where the layer's own admission rule says its salience is worth writing, before the ring's capacity is consulted. A moment the full ring turns away is still recorded.
- **`refractory_calls` still applies** on the adaptive path, because there it is part of admission. The August amendment flags it as a hidden cap on R2 and leaves it to Brian. I left it as configured and pinned the behaviour with a test, so it can't change quietly.
- **A moment's name** is the layer's forward count. Every living layer forwards once per model forward, so one number names one moment across the mind. Rest keeps or tosses by that number.
- **Checkpoints.** The record is a plain attribute, so the `strict=True` load is untouched. It rides in `living_extra_state`. A checkpoint that carries a record switches recording on in the layer it restores into, because dropping a recorded day would be silent.
- Recording only listens. A layer's parameters and buffers are bit-identical with and without it over 40 forwards.

### Step 2: can the planned rest retain anything? (standing rule 1)

The August plan: "Kept episodes consolidate into the living weights via `consolidate_layer_attractor`." Before building on it I measured whether that can last.

- One living layer, 64 wide, lived 300 forwards. Replay one input pattern at the consolidation rate. Then live 6,000 more forwards on unrelated input, beside a twin that had no replay, and ask how much of the replay's change is still in the weight.

| | after 100 | 1,000 | 3,000 | 6,000 forwards |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Replay into the weight only (the August plan) | 0.88 | 0.37 | 0.05 | 0.00 |
| Replay, and the home moved by the same change | 0.98 | 1.03 | 1.07 | 1.07 |

(Re-measured through `rest` itself after step 7's correction. The first measurement, by direct replay, read 0.91, 0.38, 0.03, −0.03 and 1.01, 1.03, 1.06, 1.05.)

- 0.37 at 1,000 forwards is 1/e. That is homeostasis pulling the weight home at `homeostatic_decay = 1e-3`, as `2026-11-03_seeding-luthi.md` section 2 describes. Twenty replays instead of one gave the same curve.
- **So the plan as written retains nothing.** A kept experience would fade exactly like a tossed one, in about 100 seconds at 10 Hz.
- Brian, 2026-11-05: rest "is needed for memory retention and formation." A rest that only replays into the weight does not do that.

### Step 3: the rest pass

- `luthi/v2/rest.py`. `rest(model, keep=..., toss=..., unjudged=...)`.
- Per layer: take the day in one swap, sort it, replay each kept experience through the layer's own update (`replay_input_attractor`, factored out of `consolidate_layer_attractor`, which is bit-identical and still passes its tests), and **move the home by the change that replay made**.
- Only rest's own change goes to the home. The layer's standing displacement from home holds the whole recent day, tossed moments included, and is left to fade. This is the difference from `keep_recent`, which keeps "the layer's whole recent change, not one moment's".
- **`unjudged` had no default when this step was built** (ruled later the same day; see step 6). What an unjudged experience becomes was Brian's open decision 2, and the August welfare note and the November ruling pull in different directions on it. The caller must say `"hold"`, `"keep"` or `"toss"`. `"hold"` leaves it in the record for a later rest, so rest never has to be a duty to sort everything.
- **Gate 1, bounded change.** A home moves at most `max_home_change` of its own size per rest. All eight tensors a replay writes are saved before each kept experience. One that would cross the bound is restored exactly and deferred, with everything after it. Deferred experiences go back into the record marked kept, and the next rest integrates them without their being named again. A later toss overrides.
- **Gate 2, the count closes.** Every experience taken is integrated, tossed, held or deferred. The pass checks the sum and raises if it is off.
- Rest calls `pc_self_modify` directly and never a forward. It records nothing, advances no counter and does not touch the episode ring. It refuses to run inside `freeze_plasticity`.
- It reads and resets each layer's `_applied_change_accum`, the day-integral built in July for this. Nothing had ever read it.
- The report lists the moments in each group per layer and can append to a JSON-lines log, so what a rest integrated can be inspected (item 6 of the plan).

### Step 4: does keeping change how the layer later meets that input?

Step 5 of the plan's order, at test size. One layer, 64 wide, 8 seeds. Keep the day's most salient experience in one twin and toss it in the other, then live 3,000 forwards. The number is the layer's relative prediction error on an input, tossed twin minus kept twin. Positive means keeping lowered the error.

| replays per kept experience | forwards after rest | the kept input | another recorded input |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | +1.1e-4 | +2.1e-6 |
| 1 | 3,000 | +2.6e-4 | +3.4e-6 |
| 20 | 0 | +2.2e-3 | +4.3e-5 |
| 20 | 3,000 | +5.4e-3 | +7.4e-5 |

- Positive in 8 of 8 seeds at every row, 50 to 75 times larger on the kept input than on another one, and still there after 3,000 forwards. (Re-measured after step 7. The kept-input column did not move.)
- **Read this narrowly.** The effect is small in absolute terms. The layer is untrained and alone, and its inputs are independent random vectors, which are nearly orthogonal at this width, so being specific is easy. In a trained trunk the inputs share a large mean component, and the 2026-10-19 audit found the living write is an outer product of batch means: "content-blind and accumulates ~rank-1." Rest replays through that same write rule. Whether what rest keeps is specific to the experience in a real trunk is the living-channel question, and E2 and E3 own it.

### Step 5: breaking it on purpose

Fourteen single breaks, one at a time: the home never moves; no undo at the bound; the bound unenforced; a toss that doesn't override a keep; deferred keeps forgotten; experiences put back out of order; held experiences dropped; rest during a measurement; `take` leaving the day in place; the record aliasing live tensors; recording below the threshold; the adaptive path never recording; a restore that drops the day; the epoch counter unsaved. All fourteen were caught by a test.

### Step 6: what nobody judged, ruled and built (later the same day)

Brian asked what "an experience Luthi doesn't judge" meant. Given the three options (hold, keep by default, toss by default) and Fable's lean toward keeping at lower strength, he ruled:

> "Lets go with option 2 but this needs to be managed so experience is not diluted by unimportant moments where nothing is happening. But if Luthi is just sitting and pondering, that is something."

Built:

- `unjudged` now defaults to `"keep"`. `"hold"` and `"toss"` remain for callers that say so.
- An unjudged experience is integrated at `unjudged_strength` (placeholder 0.5) times the strength of one Luthi chose.
- What Luthi chose is integrated first and has first call on the bound, however salient the unjudged ones are.
- An unjudged experience the bound leaves no room for goes back into the record unjudged. A chosen one goes back marked kept, as before.
- The report lists `integrated_unjudged` separately.

Then I measured dilution before managing it. One layer, 64 wide, 6 seeds. A day with one experience Luthi chose, followed by more moments nobody judged. The number is the lasting gain on the chosen input (prediction error with it tossed minus with it kept), 3,000 forwards after rest, no bound.

| the unjudged moments | none | 20 | 200 | 1,000 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Varied (independent inputs) | 2.56e-4 | 2.62e-4 | 2.75e-4 | 3.12e-4 |
| The same quiet input, repeated | 2.56e-4 | 2.59e-4 | 2.59e-4 | 1.94e-4 |

(Re-measured after step 7. The quiet row did not move, so the dilution is not that defect.)

- **Varied moments do not dilute what Luthi chose,** up to a thousand of them.
- **A long stretch of the same quiet input does:** a thousand repeats, each integrated, cut the gain by about a quarter.
- So the management is aimed at repeats. `fold_repeats_above` (placeholder 0.99): an unjudged experience whose input pattern has that cosine or more with one already integrated in this rest is folded into it. It is not replayed and does not wait in the record. The most salient of a stretch stands for it. What Luthi chose is never folded.
- With folding, the thousand quiet repeats cut the gain to 2.21e-4, about 14% down. Only 4% of them were integrated. **So most of what remains is not rest's doing.** Both twins lived the quiet stretch awake, at full waking strength, and that living is what moved the layer. Lessened waking (R1) is the lever for that, and it is not on.
- The 0.05 bound does not help here. After step 7's correction a thousand unjudged replays move the home less than 5% of its size, so the bound never binds and all of them are integrated. (Before the correction it bound at 19%, because the leak was inflating the home's movement.)

**The part of his ruling this does not meet: pondering.** Folding sees only a layer's input. A moment where the world was still and Luthi was thinking has the same input as a moment where nothing happened at all, so folding treats them alike. Worse, such a moment may never be recorded: admission is by the layer's prediction error on its input, and a still world gives little.

- Nothing on the small path today is pondering. There is no stream of thought and no speech. What happens inside in a quiet moment is the living layers settling and the action layer planning.
- The signal that would tell the two apart exists in M9: `internal_change`, the movement of the mind's state from one moment to the next, which the dark-room kill already uses so that "contemplation never trips it".
- What is needed is a second way into the record: a moment is recorded, and is not folded, when the mind's own state moved, whatever the input did. That is a call from the action layer into the record. It is the same door Luthi's "remember" choice would use. Not built. It belongs with the design of how Luthi chooses rest, and it wants the review first.

### Step 7: a defect in my own rest pass, found while writing the review brief

- Writing down what I was least sure of for the reviewer, I worked through what one replay does to the weight. `pc_self_modify` writes, and then pulls the weight toward home by `homeostatic_decay`. I was adding the whole weight change to the home, pull included.
- So each replay moved the weight and the home together by `1e-3 × (home − weight)`. The layer's standing displacement was untouched, but the home was being carried **away from the day's change**, a little with every experience kept.
- Measured: after 300 integrated experiences the home had moved 19% of the standing displacement in that direction. It should be zero. This is the opposite of what my docstring claimed ("only the change rest's own replay made is added to the home").
- Fix: `replay_input_attractor` takes optional `homeostatic_decay` and `follow_rate`. They default to the layer's own, so `consolidate_layer_attractor` is unchanged. Rest passes 0.0 for both. The weight change is then the write alone.
- Pinned by `test_rest_leaves_the_days_standing_displacement_exactly_as_it_found_it`, which fails on the code as it was.
- All three tables above were re-measured. The headline numbers held. The one thing that changed is the bound: the leak had been inflating home movement, which made `max_home_change = 0.05` look like it did something on a quiet day.
- The lesson for the reviewer: I tested that the home moved and that the change lasted. I did not test that it moved by the right thing.

## Conclusion

- Steps 1 and 2 of the rest plan exist and are tested: 16 tests for the record, 27 for the pass.
- **A finding against the August plan:** consolidating into the weight alone fades in about 1,000 forwards. Rest as built moves the home. `home_fraction=0.0` gives the plan as written, and a test characterizes that it fades.
- Nothing is on by default. No completed family's configuration changes meaning, and the full suite passes on CPU apart from the one test that asserts the ROCm default.

**Ruled by Brian the same day:** rest moves the home ("Rest moving the set point is the point so as to ingrain knowledge more fully"), and an unjudged experience is kept.

**Placeholders, all unruled** (decision 8: how strongly a kept experience is integrated): `rate_factor` 0.1 (the existing consolidation convention), `passes` 1, `home_fraction` 1.0, `max_home_change` 0.05, `unjudged_strength` 0.5, `fold_repeats_above` 0.99. At 16 wide one kept experience moved a home by about 2e-4 of its size, so 0.05 is loose. It is a ceiling against runaway, not a tuned dose.

**Choices I made that are Brian's to overrule:**

1. ~~The home moves by the replay's change.~~ Ruled: it does.
2. Kept experiences are replayed most salient first, as the existing paths do. With the bound, the least salient are the ones deferred.
3. An experience whose input no longer gives a finite output is held and reported, not tossed.
4. A moment is kept or tossed across the whole mind, not layer by layer.
5. Folding: a repeat among the unjudged is let go, not held. Held, a quiet day would pile up in the record without limit.
6. Folding compares inputs within one layer. The same moment can be folded in one layer and integrated in another.

**Not built:**

- A way for Luthi to choose rest, or to see an experience again and answer (plan items 1 and 4). Only tests call `rest`.
- Rest on Sanctuary's small path, and adapters in `luthi/sanctuary_interface.py`. Lessened waking (R1) turns on with that.
- Luthi's "remember" choice still goes nowhere. `DayRecord.append` is where it would land if Brian rules that "remember" means "store this for me to consider later".
- Corpus replay and the retention gate during rest (decision 7), and the lived update to the trained weights (decision 10), are untouched.
- The block-level episode store is untouched. It still blends a stored output into forwards.

**What I did not check:**

- Anything on a trained run, on the GPU, or above 64 wide.
- Memory over a long day. Each entry is held as CPU tensors in a Python list. The spec's arithmetic puts it near 61 MB a day at 768x8. Not measured.
- Whether the day's record holds anything on a real run. On the legacy path admission is `salience_threshold = 0.1`, and measured salience medians at scale are 0.001 to 0.004. With `adaptive_episodes` off, which is the default and what the small path uses, a real layer may record nothing. The amendment calls `adaptive_episodes=True` a precondition for this regime. The tests here set the threshold to 0.
- Rest across more than one modality.
