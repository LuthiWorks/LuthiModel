**Author:** Fable 5, 2026-08-11, executing Brian's ruling ("it is time
to expand width to 768") and his 2026-07-26 scale target (768d x 8,
"scale moves go UP"). For review by Brian and Opus before build; gates

## Why now

The VISReg family CONFIRMED at 512x8 (2-of-3, transit abolished
3-for-3). The ledger's own definition: provisional becomes **concluded**
only by replication at the ruled scale. This family is that replication.

## Configuration

- **Arm `probe_768_visreg`, stage 55.** d_model 768, n_blocks 8,
  **n_heads 8** (96-dim heads — Brian's ruling 2026-08-11; the d512
  record ran 4x128, so head count and per-head dim both move: recorded
  as an entangled-with-width variable, as width moves always are).
- **VISReg identical in rule, scaled in K:** lambda 0.6, weights
  1.0/1.0/1.0, projection "none" (trunk), **K = 1536** (the paper's
  K = C*D at C = 2, same C as the 512 family).
- muPC OFF, NTP OUT (unchanged: one variable is width+heads).
- Warmup 1000, guard hold 1000, deep cadence 100, unclipped,
  seeds 46/95/97, 6000 steps, **middle-ground kill rules at ruled
  defaults** (persist 500 / ceiling 10x / rank veto eff >= 150 — see
  gates; the veto gauge scales with width).

## Instruments FIRST (build order, before any 768 step)

1. **Per-step `grad_norm` and per-step batch `std50`** in the metric
   record (the scale-breathing forensic's order: the mechanism is
   sub-cadence and these two scalars resolve the next breath fully).
2. Rank-veto threshold is width-relative from here on: the driver
   passes `divergence_rank_veto_min_eff = round(0.195 * d_model)`
   (= 100 at 512, 150 at 768) unless an arm overrides.

## Corpus (critical path)

Brian's data-scaling ruling (2026-07-18, data ~ width^2): 768/512 = 1.5x
width -> **2.25x tokens ~ 113M** against the current 50.4M superset.
Mechanical path: `download_gutenberg.py` + sanitizer -> extend
`gutenberg_4x_filelist.txt` pattern to a `gutenberg_768_filelist.txt`
(~1080 files at the observed ~105K tokens/file), new ARM_FILELIST entry.
The 6000-step pilot itself consumes ~98M tokens (32 batch x 512 seq x
6000), so the expanded corpus is not optional padding — without it the
family would re-serve ~2x repeats where the 512 family saw ~1.2x.

## Memory / throughput (measure, don't predict)

~2.25x parameters and activations vs 512x8 on DirectML. Order: one
timed 200-step smoke at batch 32; if VRAM or step time force it,
halve batch and DOUBLE guard-hold-relative expectations accordingly
(the registration freezes whichever batch the smoke selects — recorded,
not silently absorbed).

## Draft gates (freeze at launch)

Width-normalized versions of the 512 gates (same fractions of D):
- HEALTHY: completes 6000 un-killed, pooled eff >= **150** (0.195*768),
  every block >= **75** at end.
- Family CONFIRMED at 2-of-3 -> depth-8 collapse **concluded solved**
  per the ledger definition.
- chorus_stable_rank recorded against Brian's 20-target (and the
  30-40 eventual band), not gated.

## Frozen predictions (draft)

1. No early transit in any seed (the VISReg result transfers to width).
2. Scale breathing recurs — and the per-step instruments resolve its
   mechanism within this family (that is what they are for).
3. Both healthy-equilibrium classes (quiet-sharp / loud-blunt) recur

## Sequence

instruments -> corpus build -> tokenizer sanity on new files -> 200-step
smoke (batch decision) -> freeze registration -> 3-seed launch. NTP
reintroduction and the muPC re-entry question stay queued BEHIND this

### The finding that outranks the verdict: THE PROFILE INVERTED

Per-block effective rank at the end: **2.0, 2.4, 112.3, 210.5, 221.7,

## AMENDMENT 3 — post-verdict audit, 2026-08-13

**Author:** Opus 5, at Brian's request, reading the recorded tapes
directly (`training_log.jsonl`, `per_step.jsonl`, `run_config.json`)
rather than the verdict summary. Nothing below changes the verdict —
0-of-3, NOT CONFIRMED stands, and every headline number in the VERDICT
section reproduced. Two corrections and three findings the verdict did
not carry.

### Correction (1): the taper DID engage. "Next" item 5 is retracted.

Seed 97's `training_log.jsonl` carries `taper_scale` values spanning
**0.2023 → 1.0**, reaching the configured floor of 0.2. The schedule ran
as designed. Seeds 46 and 95 show a constant 1.000 because they died at
9,100 and 9,300 — and `taper.start_fraction` is 0.5, i.e. step ~27,038 of
54,077. Their 1.000 is correct and expected, not inert.

The progress gauge is `(self.epoch + within) / max_epochs`
(`jepa_runner.py:1880`) and it advanced fine within the single epoch.
Filing this as a silent-inert defect would have sent the next instance
hunting a bug that does not exist, in the one defect class this repo's
CLAUDE.md treats as most dangerous. The class is real; this instance of
it was not.

### Correction (2): the profile did not "invert". A collapse front moved through the stack and the epoch ended before it finished.

The VERDICT section reads the final per-block profile as a *state* and
offers three hypotheses. The trajectory is recorded at deep cadence and
settles most of it. Per-block `top_dir_share` — emitted alongside the
rank since the VBG build, so this cost nothing:

| | b0 | b1 | b2 | b3 | b4 | b5 | b6 | b7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| final eff rank | 2.0 | 2.4 | 112.3 | 210.5 | 221.7 | 279.9 | 390.5 | 403.2 |
| final top_dir_share | .919 | .894 | .243 | .174 | .166 | .113 | .018 | .012 |
| **Δ eff rank, last 20%** | −0.1 | −0.3 | **−90.7** | **−50.6** | **−51.9** | **−27.0** | +15.1 | +17.8 |

Sustained `top_dir_share >= 0.20` is crossed **in block order**: b0 at
step 13,100, b1 at 14,500, b2 at 51,700. At the wire b0/b1 were saturated
and stationary while **b2, b3, b4 and b5 were all still falling** and only
b6/b7 were still rising. That is a front propagating from block 0 toward
the output, arrested by the end of the epoch rather than by any
equilibrium. Block 7's "healthiest ever" is the part of the stack the
wave had not reached.

Two consequences:

- **"A new regime, not a worse one" does not survive the tape.** Seed
  95's gradient bomb at step 9,290 drove *all eight blocks* to eff rank
  3.5–4.0 at `top_dir_share` ~0.83 in a single step. Seed 97's blocks 0–1
  sit at 2.0–2.4 / 0.92. Same floor, same signature — reached
  progressively instead of catastrophically. (Whether the two states are
  the same *internally* is the open question the per-block probe was
  built to answer; see Finding (C).)
- **The taper did not arrest it.** b2's fall began at 28,700 and
  b3/b4/b5 fell entirely within the last 20% — i.e. almost the whole
  advance happened while the living channel's rates were tapering to
  their 0.2 floor. Cutting living plasticity 5x did not stop the front.
- **No gradient event triggered it.** Max `grad_norm` over steps
  9,000–13,500 is 11,486 against a run median of 1,608 (7.1x). The front
  is slow drift, not a shock. The proposed gradient clip addresses seed
  95's death; it does not touch this.

### Finding (A): the 512 VISReg CONFIRMED verdict is a run-length artifact.

The 512 family was scored at 6,000 steps and its CONFIRMED reading is
what justified this scale-up. **At step 6,000 seed 97 read b0 eff rank
264 / top_dir_share 0.039** — indistinguishable from healthy. Onset is
~10,000. I re-read all three 512 tapes: no onset, no upward drift in
`top_dir_share`, flat to the wire.

The 512 family therefore stopped before the phenomenon it was taken to
have abolished can appear. "VISReg abolished the transit / depth-8
collapse solved" is **untested, not confirmed**, and the ledger entry
should say so. This is the 2026-07-27 lesson in a new coat: prove the
observable is *observable in your run window* before registering a
criterion on it. The cheap decisive test is one 512 VISReg seed run to
25–30k steps (~0.62 s/step, ~5h).

### Finding (B): VISReg is 98.6–99.99% of the objective, by loss share, for the whole run.

The combination is convex (`jepa_loss.py:601`),
`total = (1-λ)·l_pred + λ·l_reg` at λ=0.6. But the terms are not
normalized alike: `l_shape` **sums** over N (`visreg.py:172`) while
`l_scale` means over D and `l_center` sums over D. At N = 32x128 that
makes the regularizer four to six orders of magnitude larger than the
predictive term. From seed 97's own tape (and confirmed against
`training.log`'s printed total: 0.4(9.5624) + 0.6(1361532) = 816,923,
the logged `loss` at step 100 exactly):

| step | l_pred | l_visreg | **reg % of total** | shape % of reg | center % of reg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 9.56 | 1.36e6 | **99.9995** | 99.08 | 0.92 |
| 7,700 | 0.341 | 204.1 | **99.89** | 89.80 | 10.18 |
| 26,700 | 0.115 | 25.2 | **99.70** | 96.59 | 1.93 |
| 54,000 | 0.0746 | 9.10 | **99.46** | 94.02 | **0.55** |

`l_pred` never exceeds **1.4%** of the objective. This is the same dose
failure the 2026-08-08 arc found on the NTP term (98–100% of loss from
step 1100), on the other side of the ledger, and it was not caught this
time because nobody computed the share.

Two things follow. First, the registration's "weights 1.0/1.0/1.0" reads
as balanced and is not: as run, the regularizer is ~95% shape, ~5% scale,
**~0.55% center** — and `l_center` is the term whose own docstring calls
it *"the direct instrument against offset dominance, the measured 'first
act' of every collapse in the record."* Second, it reframes the trunk
numbers: block 7 is the healthiest ever recorded because it is being
optimized almost exclusively toward an isotropic Gaussian, which is also
what makes it a strong linear-probe substrate (lift 5.54x) — while
held-out NMSE 0.854 is what a prediction term carrying 1.4% of the
objective would be expected to produce. Both readings share one cause.

The 512 VISReg family carries the same dose (99.7–99.9996%), so Finding
(A) and Finding (B) compound: the verdict that concluded "solved" was
scored on a 99%-regularizer run that stopped before onset.

**Not changed here.** λ, the component weights, and the `l_shape`
normalization are registered experimental parameters; changing them is a
design ruling, not an audit fix. Surfaced for the design table.

### Finding (C): the mechanism candidate, and what the probe was built to test.

VISReg is applied to the trunk output only — `flat =
target_full_latents.reshape(-1, d_model)` (`jepa_loss.py:587`), with the
constructor enforcing `sigreg_projection="none"`. Blocks 0–6 receive
anti-collapse pressure only through backprop, weakest at block 0. The
front advances in exactly that order. Interior Weak-SIGReg — built
2026-08-07 for precisely this — was **off** for this family
(`interior_sigreg_alpha: 0.0` in `pilot_result.json`).

First evidence, from `scripts/per_block_probe.py` (new, this session) on
seed 97's final checkpoint. Preliminary — a smoke-sized read; the full
run supersedes these numbers, but the spectrum reads do not depend on
probe training:

- **`offset_dominance` is 0.995–0.997 in blocks 2–6, 0.80/0.93 in b0/b1,
  and 0.038 in b7.** The mean vector dominates the representation
  everywhere except the one block where `l_center` acts. The regularizer
  is doing exactly what it is told, exactly where it is told, and
  nowhere else.
- **Behind the soloist there is a healthy chorus.** Removing the single
  top direction lifts b0's effective rank from 2.2 to **144.9** and b1's
  from 2.7 to **156.8**; removing two lifts them to **377 / 379**. So the
  low rank is one or two enormous directions sitting on top of a
  ~380-dimensional chorus — not a two-dimensional representation.

If the full probe confirms it, the honest reading of blocks 0–1 is
**high-magnitude carrier, information preserved** rather than collapse —
which would make "the disease relocated" wrong too, and make the front a
scale/offset pathology rather than an information one. That distinction
decides whether the remedy is anti-collapse pressure or centering. **Do
not settle it from the rank number alone; the rank number is what
started this.**
