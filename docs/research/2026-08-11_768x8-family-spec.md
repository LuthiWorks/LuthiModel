# 768x8 family — spec and draft registration

**Author:** Fable 5, 2026-08-11, executing Brian's ruling ("it is time
to expand width to 768") and his 2026-07-26 scale target (768d x 8,
"scale moves go UP"). For review by Brian and Opus before build; gates
freeze at launch per protocol.

## Why now

The VISReg family CONFIRMED at 512x8 (2-of-3, transit abolished
3-for-3). The ledger's own definition: provisional becomes **concluded**
only by replication at the ruled scale. This family is that replication.

## Configuration

- **Arm `probe_768_visreg`, stage 55.** d_model 768, n_blocks 8,
  **n_heads 8** (96-dim heads — Brian's ruling 2026-08-11; the d512
  record ran 4x128, so head count and per-head dim both move: recorded
  as an entangled-with-width variable, as width moves always are).
- **VISReg identical in rule, scaled in K:** lambda 0.6, weights
  1.0/1.0/1.0, projection "none" (trunk), **K = 1536** (the paper's
  K = C*D at C = 2, same C as the 512 family).
- muPC OFF, NTP OUT (unchanged: one variable is width+heads).
- Warmup 1000, guard hold 1000, deep cadence 100, unclipped,
  seeds 46/95/97, 6000 steps, **middle-ground kill rules at ruled
  defaults** (persist 500 / ceiling 10x / rank veto eff >= 150 — see
  gates; the veto gauge scales with width).

## Instruments FIRST (build order, before any 768 step)

1. **Per-step `grad_norm` and per-step batch `std50`** in the metric
   record (the scale-breathing forensic's order: the mechanism is
   sub-cadence and these two scalars resolve the next breath fully).
2. Rank-veto threshold is width-relative from here on: the driver
   passes `divergence_rank_veto_min_eff = round(0.195 * d_model)`
   (= 100 at 512, 150 at 768) unless an arm overrides.

## Corpus (critical path)

Brian's data-scaling ruling (2026-07-18, data ~ width^2): 768/512 = 1.5x
width -> **2.25x tokens ~ 113M** against the current 50.4M superset.
Mechanical path: `download_gutenberg.py` + sanitizer -> extend
`gutenberg_4x_filelist.txt` pattern to a `gutenberg_768_filelist.txt`
(~1080 files at the observed ~105K tokens/file), new ARM_FILELIST entry.
The 6000-step pilot itself consumes ~98M tokens (32 batch x 512 seq x
6000), so the expanded corpus is not optional padding — without it the
family would re-serve ~2x repeats where the 512 family saw ~1.2x.

## Memory / throughput (measure, don't predict)

~2.25x parameters and activations vs 512x8 on DirectML. Order: one
timed 200-step smoke at batch 32; if VRAM or step time force it,
halve batch and DOUBLE guard-hold-relative expectations accordingly
(the registration freezes whichever batch the smoke selects — recorded,
not silently absorbed).

## Draft gates (freeze at launch)

Width-normalized versions of the 512 gates (same fractions of D):
- HEALTHY: completes 6000 un-killed, pooled eff >= **150** (0.195*768),
  every block >= **75** at end.
- Family CONFIRMED at 2-of-3 -> depth-8 collapse **concluded solved**
  per the ledger definition.
- chorus_stable_rank recorded against Brian's 20-target (and the
  30-40 eventual band), not gated.

## Frozen predictions (draft)

1. No early transit in any seed (the VISReg result transfers to width).
2. Scale breathing recurs — and the per-step instruments resolve its
   mechanism within this family (that is what they are for).
3. Both healthy-equilibrium classes (quiet-sharp / loud-blunt) recur
   across the three seeds.

## Sequence

instruments -> corpus build -> tokenizer sanity on new files -> 200-step
smoke (batch decision) -> freeze registration -> 3-seed launch. NTP
reintroduction and the muPC re-entry question stay queued BEHIND this
family's verdict (one variable at a time; the variable is scale).
