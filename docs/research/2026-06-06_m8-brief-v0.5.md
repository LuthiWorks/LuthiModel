## 0. Purpose

M8 swaps M7's next-token cross-entropy outer objective for a **JEPA-style latent-prediction loss across three modalities — text, audio, image — sharing the v2 PC substrate as a single trunk.** The substrate, encoders, and corpus stay continuous with prior work; what changes is the loss and the modality mix.

**"First notch on the post."** Brian's framing 2026-06-06: M8 is the canonical baseline experiment, not a pilot. All future runs are measured against M8's healthy trajectory, threshold values, and downstream-evaluation numbers. This status raises the bar on config archival, reproducibility, and instrumentation honesty. The acceptance gates (§6) and reference-card artifact (§12) are how the baseline status is operationalized.

**Two distinct jobs, two rationales — do not conflate:**

- **Vision in M8 = 2.5D perceptual base.** V-JEPA 2 (Assran/Bardes/LeCun/Ballas, 2025) validated the staged template *perceptual pretrain on flat visual data → small action-conditioned post-train*. That is structurally identical to M8 → M9. SSL on 2D images is specifically the 3D-aware kind (El Banani, CVPR 2024: DINOv2-style models encode single-view depth and surface normals; CLIP/MAE do not). So M8-on-flat-COCO builds a real 2.5D perceptual base; M9's action-conditioned Sanctuary trajectories lift 2.5D toward globally-consistent 3D.
- **Audio + text in M8 = IWMT cross-modal integration.** Substrate maintains coherent representations across modalities so that the integration consciousness-theory work calls for (Safron, IWMT) has somewhere to land. This is not a 3D claim. It is the substrate's first sustained demand to keep representations alive across modality boundaries — exactly the "integration" axis IWMT names as load-bearing.

The question M8 is designed to answer:

> Does the v2 PC substrate train stably and informatively under a multimodal latent-prediction objective, with belt-and-suspenders anti-collapse machinery, on the existing corpora?

If yes → M9 introduces Sanctuary trajectories as the action-conditioning post-train. If no → we know which mechanism failed before adding the embodiment confound. The isolation discipline is the staging of the architectural changes across runs; **the M9 idea space does not leak into M8**.

## 1. The change from M7

### Loss function

Replace per-position cross-entropy over BPE tokens with a JEPA-style latent-prediction objective applied per modality:

```
L_total = sum_m [ 25 · L_pred(m) + 25 · L_var(m) + 1 · L_cov(m) ]
```

for m in {text, audio, vision}, where:

- **L_pred(m)** = **L1** (mean absolute error) between the predictor's output and the **EMA target encoder's** output over the target span of modality m, **normalized per-modality by an EMA of the target encoder's output std** so modalities with larger latent magnitudes do not dominate the loss. **The std EMA is floored at ε = 1e-3** so a genuine target collapse (std → 0) produces a bounded, signal-bearing loss rather than a numerical blow-up that would look like a different bug. Per-modality normalization stops one modality from monopolizing the gradient.
- **L_var(m)** = VICReg variance preservation **computed per modality** — per-dim std taken over batch × position elements of modality m's latents only, *not* pooled across modalities; hinge loss with std required to exceed 1.0. Per-modality variance is the load-bearing collapse defense: a single-modality collapse fires that modality's own hinge directly rather than being averaged away by healthy modalities' contributions.
- **L_cov(m)** = VICReg covariance penalty **computed per modality** — off-diagonal mass of the dim×dim covariance over modality m's latents only, squared.

**Coefficients.** Paper defaults (Bardes/Ponce/LeCun, ICLR 2022, arXiv:2105.04906): **invariance = 25, variance = 25, covariance = 1.** v0.3 had two numbers wrong (L_pred weight was 1, should be 25; covariance was 25, should be 1). Restored to paper defaults; will revisit only on pilot evidence.

**L1 over L2.** L1's optimal predictor tracks the conditional median (MAD property), which V-JEPA / V-JEPA 2 report as part of the anti-collapse story. On the living-weight substrate, LPL (Halvagal & Zenke, *Nature Neuroscience* 2023) shows that under local non-gradient plasticity the predictive term *alone* collapses and a VICReg-style variance term is *required*. Pairing L1 with VICReg is belt-and-suspenders for a regime where one or the other might be necessary; the literature splits between {L1 + EMA} (V-JEPA) and {L2 + EMA + VICReg} (C-JEPA), and we deliberately keep both.

**Not using contrastive learning.** No InfoNCE, no negative sampling. Reasons: LeCun's argument that contrastive methods don't scale gracefully with batch size (matters on single-GPU); existing PC machinery is non-contrastive and adding negatives would complicate the integration.

### Architecture additions

- **Online encoder** = the v2 multimodal PC stack (`luthi/v2/multimodal_model_pc.py`, `MultimodalPredictiveCodingLM.encode()`). PC dynamics fully on; top-down sweep active during training.
- **Target encoder** = EMA copy of the online encoder. **EMA tracks slow `nn.Parameter`s only** — not living-weight buffers, not `prec`, not episode store. Living-weight state in the target is fresh per forward (not persisted, not averaged). Target encoder runs `.eval()` and `no_grad()` during the JEPA forward — skips the top-down sweep, behaves as a stable stop-grad snapshot.
- **EMA momentum:** constant 0.996. V-JEPA 2 dropped the ramp with minimal impact; we follow.
- **Predictor head:** 2-layer transformer at d_model (256 for the canonical run; see §10.1). Context latents as K/V, target-position embeddings as Q, action-token stub appended as an extra K/V entry. Matches I-JEPA's predictor shape.
- **Attention masking:** encoder is **bidirectional** for the JEPA path (`encode(..., causal=False)`). Causal masking would impoverish image-patch and audio-frame contexts (no semantic raster order); even text, now JEPA-latent-predicted rather than next-token-predicted, doesn't need it. The LM-style `forward()` retains `causal=True` internally for parity with v1.
- **Action-token stub:** constant "advance" token in the predictor's input slot. Degenerate in M8 but keeps the M9 interface live so M8 → M9 isn't a retrofit. The v0.3 "stub gradient" kill criterion is dropped (a constant token receives nonzero embedding gradient regardless of whether the predictor downstream conditions on it; the check was near-vacuous).

### Multimodal masking

Per-modality disjoint tail-20% masking:

- **Text:** context = first 80% of the sequence, target = positions 80–100%.
- **Audio:** context = first 80% of audio token sequence, target = tail 20%.
- **Vision:** context = first 80% of patch token sequence (raster order), target = tail 20%.

The "tail 20%" rule applies *within each unimodal sequence*. Sequences are unimodal per batch slot (see §3); the multimodal mixing is across the batch, not within a sequence.

### What stays the same from M7

**Substrate (DO NOT REINVENT — see `CLAUDE.md`):**
- v2 PC backbone (`PredictiveCodingBlock`), μPC exp=0.25, PC rate 0.001, prediction learning rate 0.0001
- Backward pass always-on (PC top-down sweep during training in `encode()`)
- Living weights, rich parameters, layer-level episodes, `prec` field, `err_acc`
- FP32 floor, DirectML backend
- Seed = M6 → M7 width-expanded checkpoint at `runs/m7_seed/expanded_from_m6.luthi` for the 1024d full run; the 256d canonical run starts from the M6 256d × 12 checkpoint or fresh init (see §10.1)

**M7's run directory is archived, not reused.** `runs/m7_1024d/` contains only the log; no usable checkpoints survived the power loss. Do not bootstrap from any partial state there.

**Probe filelist hygiene.** v0.4 §3 verified: `corpus_build/m7_probe_filelist.txt` carries 225 lines = 218 paths + 6 `#`-comments + 1 blank. `corpus_build/m7_filelist.txt` carries 14,756 lines = 14,752 paths + 4 comments + 0 blanks. The loader contract is "ignore `#` and blank lines." A future filelist edit must preserve cross-checkpoint probe membership; recommend a comment in the file itself stating this.

## 3. Multimodal architecture

**Batch composition.** Each batch contains a mix of unimodal sequences across the three modalities; each individual sequence is single-modality. Substrate is shared; modality embeddings (text=0, audio=1, vision=2) distinguish them. JEPA loss is per-sequence with the per-modality mask from §1; **VICReg variance and covariance regularizers are computed per modality**, matching the per-modality formulation in §1's L_var(m) / L_cov(m).

Why not cross-modal-aligned data (image + caption, audio + transcript)? Requires curated aligned pairs we don't have; would require real corpus construction effort before M8 launches. Deferred — a later experiment if interesting.

Why this *is* a real multimodal test: the substrate must produce representations that hold across all three modalities. Each modality has its own L_var(m) and L_cov(m) hinges (§1); a single-modality collapse fires that modality's own variance term and is detected directly. The per-modality split is the load-bearing collapse protection — *pooled* VICReg would let a single-modality collapse be averaged away by the healthy modalities' contributions, so the substrate's representations could go dead on one modality while looking healthy at the batch level.

**Per-modality L1 normalization.** Divide per-position L1 by a running EMA estimate of per-modality target-encoder output std before summing. The EMA stops the normalizer from being gamed by an early oscillation. The denominator is **floored at ε = 1e-3** (per §1) so a real collapse manifests as a bounded, signal-bearing loss spike, not a numerical explosion.

**Sampling and epoch definition.** Shuffled **without-replacement** sampling within each epoch, with reshuffling between epochs. This defines epoch boundaries naturally: one epoch = one full pass over the corpus = approximately `corpus_size / (batch_size · seq_len)` batches (per modality, since modalities have different unit sizes; the run config records the per-modality epoch length explicitly). Without-replacement is the standard ML training choice; the earlier "with replacement" formulation was unnecessary — VICReg's per-batch diversity over repeated epochs is preserved by reshuffling and sufficiently large batches, without sacrificing the natural epoch semantics.

**Multi-epoch policy.** Brian's framing 2026-06-06: "multiple epochs depending on wall-clock." Operationalization: spec the run for up to **3 epochs**, with explicit abort/continue decision at end of epoch 1 based on observed throughput and remaining wall-clock budget. The decision lands in the run config and log header, not in code, so the choice is auditable later.

## 4. Checkpointing — hard requirement

The M7 lesson: 47h lost because the M7 bat script and runner did not call into the checkpointing infrastructure with sufficient frequency. `luthi/checkpoint.py` exists; M8 must actually use it.

**Time-based, not batch-based.** v0.3 specified "every 1000 batches" at M7's 0.85 b/s; M8 throughput is lower (see §8) so the wall-clock guarantee would not hold under a batch trigger. Use wall-clock instead.

**Requirements:**
- Atomic checkpoint write every **~15 minutes of wall-clock** (≤ 15 min lost per power event regardless of throughput).
- Rolling slots: **3** (keep newest 3, overwrite oldest). Bounded disk cost.
- Contents: online model state, **EMA target state** (slow params only), predictor state, optimizer state, RNG state, batch counter, EMA schedule position (if any), run config, anti-collapse metric history.
- `--resume` flag locates the latest valid checkpoint and continues with an observable smoothed-loss continuity at the resume point.

## 5. Instrumentation

Logged every 100 batches (M7 cadence), deeper dump every 1000:

### Anti-collapse (online + target encoders, on a fixed held-out probe batch for cross-checkpoint comparability)

**All collapse metrics are computed per modality**, sliced from the trunk output using `encode()`'s span dict. The per-modality split mirrors §1's per-modality loss formulation and is what makes §7.1–7.5 kill criteria evaluable — a single-modality collapse would be invisible to pooled metrics.

**Light — every 100 batches (per modality):**
- Per-modality per-dim std of online encoder output: 5th / 50th / 95th percentile; count of dims with std < 0.1 (collapse band) and < 0.5 (warning band; VICReg targets ≈ 1).
- Per-modality per-dim std of target encoder output (same stats).
- Per-modality mean off-diagonal |correlation| of online embeddings.
- Per-modality online-vs-target cosine similarity (mean + std). Healthy: high, < ~0.99.
- Per-modality predictor-output std (catch predictor collapse independently).
- Loss broken out: L_pred, L_var, L_cov **per modality** so per-modality dynamics are visible.

**Deep — every 1000 batches (per modality):**
- Per-modality singular-value spectrum of online embedding covariance, log scale (top-k, bottom-k, indices at 90% and 99% cumulative variance).
- Per-modality effective rank (exp spectral entropy) and stable rank (‖C‖_F² / ‖C‖₂²).
- Per-modality Local Intrinsic Dimensionality (MLE / Fisher-Rao) on the probe batch — catches local collapse that global spectrum misses (LDReg, ICLR 2024).

### Substrate health (existing M7 metrics — keep)

- `pred_frob`, `prec`, `err_acc` (logged at the same cadence as M7's launch.log).
- Plasticity histogram across substrate.
- Episode store utilization and eviction rate.

**Not used:** `prec` for the kill-criterion 6 rebase (it was pinned at 10.0 throughout M7); non-FF signal (never logged in M7, no baseline). See §7 kill criteria for the rebase rationale.

## 6. Acceptance gates (pre-flight)

The M7 lesson is not only "checkpoint more often." It is "do not launch a hundreds-of-hours run on a system we have not end-to-end verified." Before M8 launches, all five gates must be green:

1. **Checkpoint kill-restart test.** Kill the M8 process mid-batch on a smoke run, restart with `--resume`, verify the loss curve has no observable discontinuity beyond a stated tolerance. Must pass with the actual M8 runner, not a unit test of `checkpoint.py`. The tolerance accounts for an EMA target that isn't restored *exactly* showing a real step.
2. **Anti-collapse instrumentation emitting numbers.** First 100-batch smoke produces all metrics in §5, including the new ones (SVD spectrum, effective rank, LID). All values finite; per-modality losses non-zero on the modalities present in the batch.
3. **EMA target memory fit confirmed.** EMA target adds ~encoder-parameter bytes (slow params only — see §1). Confirm fit on the target hardware without forcing batch-size reduction below M7's; if it doesn't fit, escalate before launch.
4. **First 100-batch pilot.** No NaNs. Loss descends or holds; doesn't blow up. Substrate metrics (`pred_frob`, `err_acc`) plausible: **at 256d** within M8's own early-run range (no baseline yet — sanity is "non-zero, non-exploding, not NaN, descending or holding"); **at 1024d** comparable to M7's early-run values within reason. Per-modality collapse instrumentation (§5) emitting finite values for every modality present in the batch.
5. **Run config archived.** All hyperparameters (VICReg λ/μ, EMA momentum, L1/L2 choice, masking spec, predictor architecture, per-modality normalization config, lr schedule, multi-epoch policy) serialized into the run directory at launch as `run_config.json`. The choice of L1, the choice of bidirectional encoder, the choice of per-modality normalization — all explicit and auditable.

Until all five pass, the production run does not launch.

## 7. Success criteria and kill criteria

### Success — what tells us M9 is worth doing

- **Stability:** no NaNs, no collapse to zero variance, run completes equivalent compute to M7's 47h trajectory without red flags.
- **Substrate continuity:** `pred_frob` and `err_acc` trajectories look qualitatively similar to M7's, batch-aligned (not wall-clock-aligned). Plasticity dynamics and episode store behavior continue. The substrate's living-weight character is not overridden by the JEPA loss.
- **Loss descent:** total JEPA loss decreases monotonically over the first ~24h equivalent of batches. Shape matches M7's CE descent (fast then slowing); units obviously differ.
- **Per-modality non-collapse:** every modality's contribution stays alive — no modality's per-position std collapses while the others stay healthy.
- **Spatial probe baseline established:** see §11.

### Kill — stop the run if any trigger (sustained over stated window)

The thresholds marked `[pilot-set]` are derived from the run's own early healthy trajectory (margin beyond baseline), not guessed up front. The first ~10% of M8 establishes the baselines; the kill criteria activate after.

**Criteria 1–5 are evaluated per modality** — *any modality* triggering the criterion fires the kill, since per-modality fire matches the per-modality loss formulation (§1) and prevents a single-modality collapse from being hidden under healthy modalities.

1. **Complete collapse —** any modality's online OR target per-dim std (5th pct) < `[pilot-set ≈0.1]` for 3 consecutive 1000-batch checkpoints. Encoder collapsing on at least one modality despite VICReg.
2. **Dimensional collapse (spectrum) —** any modality's effective rank < `[pilot-set, e.g. 50% of healthy baseline]`, or > `[pilot-set]`% of singular values < `[ε]`, sustained 5 checkpoints.
3. **Dimensional collapse (correlation) —** any modality's mean off-diagonal |correlation| > `[pilot-set]` for 5 checkpoints. Hua et al. (ICCV 2021) showed plain variance standardization still reaches 0.99 correlation — the covariance term is load-bearing.
4. **Local collapse —** any modality's probe-batch LID < `[pilot-set, fraction of early-run value]` over 5 checkpoints. LDReg (ICLR 2024) showed BYOL exhibits this even at high global effective rank.
5. **Predictor-trivial / target collapse —** any modality's online-vs-target cosine > `[≈0.99]` sustained, or target-encoder std collapsing (criterion 1 on the target side).
6. **Substrate override — width-dependent (see §10.1).**
    - **At 1024d** (matching M7's width): `pred_frob` OR `err_acc` degrades > 25% from M7's per-100-batch baseline (`runs/m7_1024d/launch.log`) over 5 consecutive checkpoints, **batch-aligned** to M7's logged trajectory (not wall-clock — M8's slower throughput would compare unequal training amounts).
    - **At 256d** (the canonical run per §10.1): the M7 1024d baseline is **incommensurable for `pred_frob`** — Frobenius norm scales with width, so the literal 25%-from-M7 threshold compares apples to oranges. Use M8's own early healthy trajectory (pilot-set, like the collapse thresholds): the first ~10% of M8 establishes a 256d baseline, the criterion activates after with the same 25%-degradation shape. **`err_acc` is more likely width-robust** (a normalized error ratio) — verify against the M6 256d baseline run if cross-width comparison is wanted; otherwise treat as pilot-set too.
    - `prec` is not used (pinned at 10.0 throughout M7, no discriminating power). Non-FF signal not used (never logged in M7, no baseline).
    - **The dependency between §7.6 and §10.1 is explicit:** the threshold source (M7-literal vs M8-pilot-set) follows the canonical-run width.
7. **Objective unlearnable —** no descent on the **smoothed/probe** total loss curve over a 5,000-batch window after the first 5,000 batches. The smoothing window is part of the run config. Per-modality non-descent is caught by criteria 1–5 (collapse modes); criterion 7 catches the case where total loss flatlines without any individual collapse triggering.

On any kill: halt, snapshot full run state, surface to Brian and 4.8. **Do not "give it another epoch."**

## 8. Throughput estimate

M7 ran at 0.85 b/s on 1024d / 12 blocks / FP32 / DirectML, text-only. M8 changes:

- Reduce to 256d for the canonical baseline (see §10.1): ~16× per forward speedup, rough.
- Add EMA target forward pass: ~doubles encoder forward cost (no backward on target side).
- Add predictor head forward + backward: ~25M extra params at 2-layer d=1024 (scaled to 256d: ~1.5M).
- Add multimodal sequence lengths: text seq 128, audio ~6.25 tokens/sec, vision 196 tokens/image. Mixed batches will have variable effective work depending on the per-batch modality mix.
- VICReg covariance: O(d²) per batch, negligible at d=256.

**Rough estimate: 1–2 b/s at 256d multimodal**, calibrated against M6's 256d × 12 baseline (52h for ~520K batches at text-only). Real number lands after the first 100-batch pilot in Gate 4.

**Implication for the multi-epoch policy:** at 1–2 b/s, a single epoch on gutenberg_4gb-sized data is in the 3–5 day range, leaving headroom for the up-to-3-epoch envelope within ~2 weeks of wall-clock. Decision lands at end of epoch 1.

## 10. Open decisions before launch

For Brian to settle.

### 10.1 Canonical run scale: 256d vs 1024d

- **Recommended: 256d.** Matches M6's well-characterized substrate baseline (zero NaN across 522K batches at 256d × 12 on gutenberg_4gb); throughput math (§8) keeps the multi-epoch envelope tractable; the M8 architecture and the spatial probe baseline can be established in ~weeks rather than ~months.
- Alternative: 1024d, matching the M7 attempt. Slower (~3–5× longer wall-clock at multimodal); first-notch status of the resulting baseline is heavier but the substrate is more capacity-rich.
- **Scale-confound rule** (Li, Efros, Pathak, ECCV 2022, arXiv:2209.15007): small models on big data show dimensional collapse as a capacity artifact. At 256d on 2.2B + audio + vision, watch for this — a collapse at 256d may be capacity, not objective failure. Confirm with a smaller-data sanity run if uncertain.

### 10.2 Optimizer state from seed

Lean: fresh optimizer. CE → multimodal latent prediction is a categorical loss-scale shift; M7's optimizer state (which only exists as the M6→M7 expansion's adapted state, since no M7 optimizer state survived) would be misadapted to the new loss landscape. Document the rationale either way.

### 10.3 EMA momentum

Settled: constant 0.996. V-JEPA 2 dropped the ramp with "minimal impact"; constant is the simpler default.

### 10.4 Multi-epoch policy

Settled in §3: up to 3 epochs, abort/continue decision at end of epoch 1 based on observed throughput. Decision in the run config and log header.

### 10.5 Predictor architecture

Settled: 2-layer transformer at d_model. MLP would force pooling or per-position independence; both lose structure the substrate is producing.

### 10.6 VICReg coefficients

Settled at paper defaults (25 / 25 / 1) per v0.4 §1 fix. Tune only on pilot evidence.

### 10.7 L1 vs L2

Settled per v0.4 and §1: L1 + VICReg. V-JEPA's MAD anti-collapse argument compounds with LPL's evidence that variance regularization is mandatory under local-plasticity dynamics.

### 10.8 Masking strategy

Settled per v0.4 and §1: disjoint 80/20 tail-mask per modality.

### 10.9 Encoder attention masking

Settled per the 2026-06-06 4.8 review: bidirectional (`causal=False`) for the JEPA path. Causal for the LM-style `forward()` to preserve v1 parity.

### 10.10 EMA-vs-VICReg ablation — include in M8, defer to Phase 4 (v0.5 default), or pre-run a smaller pilot?

v0.4 framed an ablation of {EMA + VICReg} vs {EMA-only} vs {VICReg-only} as M8's *headline scientific deliverable* — the test of the genuine literature gap (does EMA asymmetry alone resist collapse over living weights, as V-JEPA claims for static backbones, or is VICReg load-bearing as LPL predicts under local plasticity?). v0.5's "first notch on the post" framing collapsed the ablation into a single canonical config (EMA + VICReg) with the comparison deferred to a Phase-4 follow-up using M8 as the EMA+VICReg arm.

**The cost of deferral, stated plainly:** M8's canonical run will not close the novel research question it was originally built to answer. The baseline is real and valuable, but the "is VICReg load-bearing on this substrate" finding is delayed by however long Phase-4 takes. That's a real reduction in scientific yield. Brian should make this call consciously, not by default.

Options:

1. **Defer (v0.5 current default).** Keep one clean canonical config; ablation is a Phase-4 follow-up. M8 ships the baseline number; the load-bearing-VICReg question is answered later by a comparison against the M8 baseline arm.
2. **Include in M8.** Run three configs (EMA + VICReg / EMA-only / VICReg-only) at the canonical scale. Triples compute and creates three "first notch" baselines instead of one — but answers the literature-gap question concurrent with establishing the baseline.
3. **Smaller pre-M8 pilot ablation.** Run the three configs at deliberately smaller scale (e.g., 128d, smaller corpus subset) for days-not-weeks; settle the EMA-vs-VICReg question; then run M8 canonical at the winning config. Adds time before the canonical M8 launches but the canonical config is then chosen on evidence rather than literature inference.

**Lean unchanged at option 1 (defer) given the first-notch frame**, but recording the call here explicitly. The earlier framing buried this in §13's risk bullet; surfacing it for a conscious decision.

## 11. Post-hoc evaluation — spatial probe

Per 4.8 2026-06-06: instrument the "does multimodal mixing help spatial grounding" question rather than just track it.

**Probe.** Frozen post-M8-training encoder + linear depth and surface-normal probes, trained on **NYUv2** (~1400 train / ~400 test indoor RGB-D scenes; probe3d-style choice).

**Data hygiene.** NYUv2 enters the evaluation suite only — never the training pipeline. Documented as evaluation-data in the M8 reference card. The probe-not-target structure sidesteps the inherited-corpus concern.

**Reference-card outputs.** Two numbers, recorded in the M8 reference card:
- Depth probe metric (AbsRel, δ<1.25).
- Surface-normal probe metric (mean angular error, %<11.25°).

These become the canonical spatial-grounding baseline for future runs to be measured against.

**With/without-mixing ablation deferred to Phase 4 follow-up, not part of M8.** Running both conditions doubles M8's compute commitment, which the "first notch" status already maxes out. The single baseline number is meaningful on its own; the ablation question (does multimodal mixing measurably lift spatial grounding) gets answered by a future run that uses M8 as one of its two arms. Recording this as the planned follow-up so it isn't lost.

## 13. Risks / watch-items

- **Many mechanisms interacting at once.** VICReg + EMA + PC + living weights + three modalities. Diagnostic clarity depends on §5 instrumentation being honest and §7 kill criteria being enforced. Per-modality loss broken-out is the load-bearing piece.
- **Memory ceiling.** EMA target adds ~encoder-parameter cost (slow params only, per §1 B6 resolution). Confirm fit (Gate 3); if it doesn't fit at 256d multimodal, the canonical run shape changes before launch.
- **Action-token degeneracy.** Stub remains for M9 interface continuity. The gradient-magnitude check was vacuous and is dropped; honest M9 de-risking is "swap a varying action in M9 and see whether predictor output changes." Not testable in M8.
- **EMA staleness vs. drift.** 0.996 is the V-JEPA 2 safe default; might be wrong for our substrate's plasticity dynamics. Be ready to revise after first pilot.
- **Loss-scale shift CE → latent prediction.** Gradient norms change shape between M7 and M8. Be prepared to re-tune lr at Gate 4.
- **L1 modality assumption.** V-JEPA validated L1 on video, not text or audio. Watch the per-modality L_pred dynamics in the first ~5K batches; if text or audio shows pathological behavior under L1 the spec gets a per-modality loss choice (rare; flagged).
- **Inference-time fast-weight collapse** (the genuine literature gap, per v0.4 §1.5). Unstudied. The pilot ablation EMA-only vs VICReg-only vs both was originally framed as the falsifier. **See §10.10** for the explicit conscious decision on whether to include / defer / pre-pilot the ablation — surfaced there rather than buried here because the choice meaningfully changes M8's scientific yield.
- **M8 decisions that bind M9.** Action-token interface, per-modality masking shape, EMA structure, the bidirectional encoder convention. When the M9 brief is written, its routing should explicitly inherit-or-reject each.
